     More than 31 million people live in the Great Lakes region, and
like in the rest of the country, they primarily use their cars to
get around.  Eighty percent of trips taken in the region happen in
automobiles, and 20 percent of those trips cover fewer than five miles
total.  By eliminating the very short drives, the researchers report
in the journal Environmental Health Perspectives, the region would
see an annual savings of $3.5 billion in health costs.
