     Say hello to MasterCard's new Display Card.  Manufactured by
NagraID Security, the card looks and functions like your typical credit
or debit card, except for a few new features, such an embedded LCD
display and touch-sensitive buttons that will allow you to create a
One-Time Password (OTP) for added authentication.
     After more than a month of searching, scientists using NASA's
rover Curiosity to study Mars' atmosphere have found no evidence that
the planet most like Earth in the solar system has methane, a gas
tied to biological processes.  The finding adds a new twist into a
puzzling story about methane on Mars, which previously was detected
by ground-based telescopes and orbiting spacecraft.
     Raccoons, skunks, possums and certain other animals have long
been city dwellers, but now larger wild carnivores are moving into
urban areas, according to a symposium presented today at EcoSummit
2012, an international conference held in Columbus, Ohio.  Leading
the way are coyotes, which have established a territory just five
miles from Chicago O'Hare International Airport.  They appear to be
paving the way for other large mammalian carnivores.
