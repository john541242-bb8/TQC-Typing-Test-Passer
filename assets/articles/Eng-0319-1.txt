     Vincent van Gogh's "Flowers in a blue vase" painting have changed
color over time mysteriously, and now scientists have figured out
why:  A chemical reaction between the paint and the protective varnish
supposedly applied to the painting after the artist's death in 1890
turned his bright yellow flowers an orange-gray color.  The chemical
degradation occurred right at the interface between the paint and
the varnish, the researchers added.
     Over the past 50 years, the salty parts of the oceans have become
saltier and the fresh regions have become fresher and the degree of
change is greater than scientists can explain.  Researchers are heading
out into one particularly salty ocean region, in the middle of the
North Atlantic Ocean, in the hopes of better understanding what drives
variation in salinity in the upper ocean.
