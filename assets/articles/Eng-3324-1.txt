     With winter solstice this morning marking the start of longer
days to come, sunny skies over much of the country are a welcome sight.
The southwest however will have to wait as heavy precipitation from
yesterday's eastern storm is lingering over Mississippi and Alabama.
The cold storm is expected to bring hail north of New Orleans and
increased flood risks as well as the potential for isolated tornadoes.
Over in northern New Mexico snowfall has started and will spread to
the south tonight and Friday with the heaviest falls bringing much
needed snow to the northern mountains and along the eastern slopes
of the central mountain chain.
     By analyzing bones and teeth from more than 30 horses in Siberia
and Europe dating back as many as 35,000 years, researchers found
that six shared a gene associated with a type of leopard spotting
seen in modern horses.  Until now, scientists only had DNA evidence
of monochrome horses, such as bay and black.  One prominent example
that has generated significant debate over its inspiration is the
25,000-year-old painting, "The Dappled Horses of Pech-Merle" in France,
showing white horses with black spots.
