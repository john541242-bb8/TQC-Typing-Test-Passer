     Bananas were first domesticated 7,000 years ago in Southeast Asia.
As people migrated, and crossed their own plants with other species
along the way, bananas gradually became seedless, delicious and totally
sterile.  Instead of multiplying through sexual reproduction, which
mixes up the gene pool, bananas are cultivated through vegetative
propagation, which involves simply cutting off a section of one plant
to grow on its own.  It's the same process used to grow several other
major African crops, including cassava, sweet potatoes and yams.
