     CITES said elephants have been slaughtered by groups from Chad
and the Sudan in recent weeks, taking advantage of the dry season.
The poached ivory is believed to be traded for money, weapons and
munition, fueling conflicts in neighboring countries.  The UN agency
said it will contact the ministers responsible for forests and wildlife
from Cameroon, Chad, the Central African Republic, the Democratic
Republic of the Congo and Sudan to offer anti-poaching support.
     Earlier research found that signature whistles are unique for each
dolphin, with the marine mammals essentially naming themselves and
communicating other basic information.  A signature dolphin whistle
in human speak, might be comparable to, "Hi, I'm George, a large,
three-year-old dolphin in good health who means you no harm."
     Reusing beads is not just good for the environment, it's good
for the American economy. Arc Enterprises, a branch of the nonprofit
Arc of Greater New Orleans, employs mentally challenged workers to
sort the beads.  The beads are then sold at a discount.  That means
less petroleum-based plastic beads have to be imported from overseas.
