     Running 26.2 miles may make you sweaty and sore, but it won't give
you a heart attack, found a new study.  The finding offers a comforting
counterpoint to the media frenzy that often follows when someone drops

cases, the new study found, athletes had a preexisting heart condition
that would've likely become a problem even if victims had never signed
up for a long-distance event.
