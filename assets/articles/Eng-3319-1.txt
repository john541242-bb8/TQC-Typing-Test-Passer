     We humans are noisy, and birds don't like it.  Some birds change
their tunes to deal with the din.  Now it seems that other birds are
just packing up and moving away.  A new study in today's issue of PLoS
ONE finds that this strategy is particularly true of larger songbirds
such as western tanagers (above), whose low-frequency calls are simply
drowned out by human-made ruckus.
