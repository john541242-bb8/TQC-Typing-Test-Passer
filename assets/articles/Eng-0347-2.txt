     Water makes up more than half of your body weight and a person
can't survive for more than a few days without it.  Why?  Your body
has lots of important jobs and it needs water to do many of them.
For instance, your blood, which contains a lot of water, carries oxygen
to all the cells of your body.  Without oxygen, those tiny cells would
die and your body would stop working.
