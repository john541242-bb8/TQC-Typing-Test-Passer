     Once detonated the barb of water does two things:  First, it slices
through the exterior casing of the IED.  Metal, wood or plastic, the
Stingray will cut through them all.  Second, once the blade of water
enters the explosive device, it courses this way, shredding wires,
detonators and any other bomb parts it comes in contact with.
