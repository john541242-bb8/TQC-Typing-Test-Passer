     Five federal charges were filed against Shahzad on Tuesday in U.S.
District Court:  attempting to use a weapon of mass destruction, acts
of terrorism transcending national boundaries, use of a destructive
device in connection with criminal violence, transporting and receiving
explosives, and damaging and destroying property by means of fire.
     Pomrenke and his crewmates will be featured on the new Discovery
Channel show Bering Sea Gold.  "It's been 20 below for I forget how
many days straight," he said.  Residents have enough heating oil to
keep their houses warm, but not having enough gas for vehicles would
be a problem, Pomrenke said.  He remains optimistic that the fuel will
arrive soon, though.  "I have confidence in 'em," he said.  "I hope they
make it.  Otherwise it's going to make life out here really miserable."
     I have a dog, Leyla; I pick up a lot of dog waste.  Leyla and I go
to dog parks a lot.  The garbage cans, which contain picked up dog
waste, stink to high heaven.  Especially in the summer.  But never
did it occur to me that the pool could be used for something more than
catching flies.  Wouldn't you know it?  Right here in Massachusetts,
in the next town over, someone did think of a useful way to reuse dog
turns.  Artist Matthew Mazzotta has come up with simple system that
converts dog waste into energy that can be used to generate electricity.
