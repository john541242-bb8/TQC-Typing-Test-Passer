     However, there can be no stranger sight on this island than its
mud volcanoes.  'Erupting' at intervals with oozing streams of thick,
sticky mud and flammable gas, mud volcanoes are just plain weird.  Mud
volcanoes are remarkably uncommon, found in only about twenty countries
around the world, but above all, they're highly unpredictable.  On a
recent visit to Taiwan's southernmost county of Pingtung, we made a
quick diversion off the road south towards Kenting to visit the Liushan
Mud Volcano, not far from the town of Donggang.
