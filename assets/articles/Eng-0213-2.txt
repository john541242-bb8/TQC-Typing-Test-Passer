     Hietz and his team point out that nitrogen dioxide concentrations
are already high above both forests they studied and that similar
values have been recorded 21%, 26%, and 12% of tropical Africa, Asia,
and America, respectively.  They speculate that the excess nitrogen
on Barro Colorado Island comes from local sources.  Much of it probably
originates in fossil-fuel emissions blowing in from Panama City, 40
kilometers away, and the plethora of ships that pass the island en
route to the Panama Canal.
