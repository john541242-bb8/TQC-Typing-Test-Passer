     President Barack Obama's re-election Tuesday night means NASA
will likely continue along its current path, working to get astronauts
to an asteroid by 2025 among other goals.  A change of course for the
space agency was possible had Mitt Romney won the presidency, as the
Republican candidate pledged to reassess NASA's path forward.  But
we'll never know what a Romney-revised path may have looked like,
for President Obama won the day.
