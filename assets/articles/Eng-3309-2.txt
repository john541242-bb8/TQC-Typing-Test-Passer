     Not satisfied with that, however, Amin looked at how to make
the ZnO nanorods printable, which would get him to the end result
faster.  For that, he had to get the ZnO nanorods off of the paper.
He used ultrasound to "scrape" off the rods and then mixed them with a
solution to create an ink.  Using an ordinary silkscreen printing
machine, he printed the ZnO onto a paper treated in a similar way to
the one he used to grow the crystals.
