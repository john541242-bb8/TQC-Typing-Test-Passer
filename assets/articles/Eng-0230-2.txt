     Although calling the spacecraft a laboratory might suggest it
will stay in one place, Curiosity actually is a rover that will travel
some 12 miles inside Gale Crater during its 23-month mission.  The
size of a car or small SUV, the rover weighs nearly a ton and its
scientific payload is 10 times more massive than the instrument sets
taken to Mars by previous rovers.
     Mawson landed at Commonwealth Bay on January 8, 1912, building a
complex of huts at Cape Denison that stand to this day.  Three tourist
ships that have been attempting to reach the cape as part of 100-year
commemorations of the voyage had to ditch their plans on Wednesday
due to rare conditions caused by the mammoth iceberg, an official said.
     Yesterday we celebrated the birth (in 1828) of legendary science
fiction novelist Jules Verne, who penned such classic works as Twenty
Thousand Leagues Under the Sea and From the Earth to the Moon.  The
latter is one of the earliest depictions of space travel in popular
fiction, featuring a capsule shot out of a cannon.
