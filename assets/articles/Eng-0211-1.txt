     Later this month, during the numbingly cold Mongolian winter,
holes will be drilled in the ice on the Tuul River by English-Mongolian
engineering firm ECOS & EMI.  Water will spurt from beneath the ice
then freeze and increase the thickness of the ice cap.  Repeating this
process will create a massive block of ice several meters thick.  As
the block slowly melts during the torrid steppe summer, Mongolians
hope this feat of geo-engineering will reduce the urban heat island
effect and fight rising temperatures.
