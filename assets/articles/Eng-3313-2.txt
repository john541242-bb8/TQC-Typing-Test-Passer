     Experts say hot days certainly take a toll on our bodies, but
they can also test our tempers.  Many people feel a little hotheaded
when the mercury rises, said Nancy Molitor, an assistant professor of
clinical psychiatry and behavioral science at Northwestern University
Feinberg School of Medicine.  In fact, hot and especially humid weather
is known to be associated with increases in aggression and violence, as
well as a lower general mood, Molitor said.
