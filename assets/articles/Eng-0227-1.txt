     More than 100 Beluga whales remain trapped between ice floes in
the Chukotka region of Russia's Far East as bad weather hampers the
rescue operation, the emergencies ministry said Tuesday.  "The weather

where the Belugas are trapped," a spokesman of the ministry's Chukotka
branch told AFP by telephone.
