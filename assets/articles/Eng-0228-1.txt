     Scientists say rare fossils found in Tasmania's south-east prove
that an ancient species of prehistoric animal did exist throughout
Australia.  The dicynodont was an early ancestor of modern-day mammals
and lived about 250 million years ago.  Roughly the size of a cow, the
plant-eating animal had two tusks and a horny beak.
