     Same-store sales, or sales at stores open at least a year, are
a key indicator of retailers' performance.  The data are also important
to the overall recovery, since consumer spending fuels two-thirds of
the economy.  The weakness in April data was in sharp contrast to the
previous month.  March's report showed sales increased 9.1% over last
year, the biggest monthly gain since Thomson began keeping records in
2000.  The March data got a boost from unusually warm weather, Easter
shopping and improved consumer confidence, Thomson said in its report.
