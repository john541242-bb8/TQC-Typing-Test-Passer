     Adenoviruses are some of the many viruses responsible for causing
colds and stomach ailments in people.  In the new study, 67 obese and
57 normal-weight children ranging in age from 8 to 18 were tested
for the presence of antibodies against adenovirus-36 in their blood.
Researchers led by Jeffrey Schwimmer, a pediatric gastroenterologist
at the University of California, San Diego, and Rady Children's Hospital
San Diego, found antibodies in 19 of the children.
