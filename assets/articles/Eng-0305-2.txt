     Dark skies are often difficult to come by, especially if you,
like the majority of the world's population, live near a sprawling
metropolis.  The increasingly acute scourge of light pollution is a
pressing issue not only for amateur and professional astronomers,
but for anyone who wants to look up on a cloudless night.  But deep
inside Yosemite National Park, astronomers flock with their increasingly
sophisticated array of astronomical equipment to stare deep into space
and bathe in the starry spectacle above them.
