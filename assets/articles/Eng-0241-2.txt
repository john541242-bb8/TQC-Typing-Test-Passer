     Visitors can walk along paths bordered by tropical plants and
tall trees, and enjoy the view of a natural-looking manmade waterfall.
The path then leads visitors to an air-conditioned basement, where a
14-foot long aquarium features fish and other aquatic creatures from
the Amazon River.  A series of smaller aquariums housing tetra fish
and piranhas are their neighbors, creating a fascinating atmosphere
to charm young and old.
