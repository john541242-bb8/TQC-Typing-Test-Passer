     Unlike a typical heart attack, which occurs because a blockage
has formed in one of the heart's arteries, preventing blood flow, no
blockage occurs in broken heart syndrome.   Instead, doctors believe
that the adrenaline surge can cause the lower chamber of the heart
to go into temporary paralysis, which forces the upper chamber to
overwork, causing typical heart attack symptoms.
