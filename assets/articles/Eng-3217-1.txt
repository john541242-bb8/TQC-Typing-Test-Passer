     Esperanza, which means hope in Spanish, is the name of Ariel
Ticona's baby girl.  She is also believed to be the first child born to
any of the 33 miners during their nightmarish weeks buried underground.
Esperanza was born Tuesday in a hospital about an hour's drive down
a winding mountain road from the mine where her father is trapped.
     Within the next 30 years, a major earthquake with a magnitude
of 6.7 or higher is expected to hit San Francisco.  That's why the
Bay Bridge, which connects San Francisco and Oakland, is undergoing
major seismic renovations.  During 1989's Loma Prieta Earthquake,
which registered 6.9 on the Richter scale, a section of the Bay Bridge
collapsed, killing a motorist.  Since then, major studies were conducted
to determine if California's largest bridges were seismically safe.
