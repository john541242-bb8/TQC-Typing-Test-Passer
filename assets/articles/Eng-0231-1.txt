     Don't worry Hemingway, the snows of Kilimanjaro aren't melting
because of deforestation.  Climate scientists from the University of
Innsbruck, Austria, found that changes in land cover didn't have a
major influence on glacier cover on Kilimanjaro, a massive extinct
volcano in northeast Tanzania and Africa's highest mountain.
