     While the eradication of polio is seemingly within reach, this
is not the time to relax, Dr. Shulman warns.  Most countries only
investigate the possibility of poliovirus outbreaks when paralytic
cases appear in the human population.  But this doesn't take into
account a potential problem posed by the live virus vaccine.  Over
time, the vaccine can mutate, and even a 1 percent genomic change
in the virus permits the virus to behave like a wild poliovirus.
