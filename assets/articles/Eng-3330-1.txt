     What is known is that Sarahsaurus lived about 190 million years ago
during the Early Jurassic Period.  The dino was 14 feet long and weighed
about 250 pounds.  It possessed physical traits usually associated with
gigantic animals.  For example, its thigh bones were long and straight
like pillars, yet were not much larger than a human's thigh bones.
Sarahsaurus shows that sauropodmorphs started out small and later
evolved to a very large size.
