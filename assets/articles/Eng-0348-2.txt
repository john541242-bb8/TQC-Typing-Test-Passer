     Every state and territory in the United States has now established
an Early Hearing Detection and Intervention program to identify before
3 months of age every child born with a permanent hearing loss, and to
provide intervention services before 6 months of age.  If your baby
doesn't have this screening, or was born at home or a birthing center,
it's important to have a hearing screening within the first 3 weeks of
life.  If your baby does not pass the hearing screening, it doesn't
necessarily mean there's a hearing loss.
