     African elephants buzz off when bees are in the area.  And once
stung, precautious pachyderms never forget to behave around the bee
hive.  Biologist Lucy King put elephants' fear of bees to use guarding
farmers' crops in Kenya.  Not only do the bees protect the fields,
their honey provides a secondary income for the farmers.
