     Paleoanthropologist Alan Walker of Penn State University thinks
that even if a human and a chimp were somehow evenly matched in size,
chimpanzees wind up using all of their muscle strength, whereas humans
tend to hold back.  Relative to body mass, chimpanzees have less grey
matter in their spinal cords than humans have.
