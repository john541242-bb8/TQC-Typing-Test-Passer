     Artist and composer Ben Grosser, who is working on an MFA in New
Media at the University of Illinois, has developed a robot that's able
to hear the world around it and use those sounds to create a painting.
The Interactive Robotic Painting Machine has a microphone that captures
surrounding sound and a genetic algorithm designed to transform those
sounds into computer code ultimately drives the robot's paintbrush in
three dimensions, controlling how much paint to put on the brush and
how much pressure to apply to canvass.
