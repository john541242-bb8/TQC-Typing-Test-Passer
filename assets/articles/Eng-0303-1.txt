     Kononenko served as commander of Expedition 31, but will pass the
post on to fellow Russian cosmonaut Gennady Padalka in an official
change-of-command ceremony Saturday (June 30), prior to his departure.
Padalka arrived at the space station in mid-May with NASA astronaut
Joe Acaba and Russian cosmonaut Sergei Revin and will remain aboard
the orbiting outpost until September.  They will be joined by three
more crewmembers later in July.
     As deforestation has accelerated in the Brazilian Amazon over
the last 40 years, scientists have been watching for an equally rapid
rate of extinctions among animals that are losing their habitats.  But
so far, no species have disappeared from the region as a whole, and
only a small percentage of those predicted to be at risk have gone
extinct on a local basis.  Instead, there has been a delay between
forest loss and species loss, putting the Amazon in debt.
     But past studies have shown that women who drink moderately have
higher bone density than non-drinkers or heavy drinkers.  Now, the new
study suggests why:  Alcohol appears to reduce bone loss in middle-age
women by suppressing the rate at which their bones shed old cells.  In
line with previously observed trends, the women in the study who drank
more alcohol had denser hip bones than those who drank less.
