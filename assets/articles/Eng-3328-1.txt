     Expecting food, whale sharks might approach other boats and risk
colliding with them.  They also might be more vulnerable to poachers
who will catch and kill them, Arregadas said.  However Mayor Guaren
said that even after years of feeding, no whale sharks had turned up
dead and insisted that the animals were not dependent on feeding and
could still find food on their own.
