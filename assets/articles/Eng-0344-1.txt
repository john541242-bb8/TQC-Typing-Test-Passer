     Lightly oil the bottom only of the pan or line with parchment
paper.  The torte bakes best when it clings to the sides of the pan.
Gently pour the mixture into the pan.  Bake in an oven preheated to
350 degrees F for about 30 minutes or until a knife inserted in the
torte comes out clean.  Remove pan from oven and gently slip a knife
around the sides of the pan before releasing the springform.  Serve
with fresh whipped cream and sun-ripened raspberries in the summer,
or dried organic cherries in the winter.

caused by chlorine from man-made chemicals called chlorofluorocarbons,

like spray cans.  CFCs, which destroy ozone, are believed to linger
in the stratosphere for decades.
