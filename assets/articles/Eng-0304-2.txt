     New Yorkers will be treated to a special sight Thursday evening
(July 12):  It's one of two days a year when the setting sun aligns
perfectly with Manhattan's street grid.  As the sun sets on the Big
Apple, it will light up both the north and south sides of every cross
street.  The event has been dubbed "Manhattanhenge" for the way it
turns New York City into a Stonehenge-like sun dial.
