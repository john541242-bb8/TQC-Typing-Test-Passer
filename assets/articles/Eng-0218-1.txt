     The way Bigelow sees it, China, which has no debt, cash reserves

national support, will lay claim to raw materials on the moon in about
15 years.  The land grab will occur despite an international treaty
barring ownership of extraterrestrial bodies, concludes Bigelow, who 
points out that China's growing global economic influence will make 
it less likely other countries will object to its lunar expansion.
