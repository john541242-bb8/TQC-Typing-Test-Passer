     New images and animated movies from NASA's Cassini spacecraft
chronicle the birth and evolution of the colossal storm that ravaged
the northern face of Saturn for nearly a year.   These new full-color
mosaics and animations show the storm from its emergence as a tiny
spot in a single image almost one year ago, on Dec. 5, 2010, through
its subsequent growth into a storm so large it completely encircled the
planet by late January 2011.
