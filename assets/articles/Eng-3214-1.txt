     Early in our nation's history, women participated in war officially
only as nurses.  Then came World War II, said historian Kara Dixon
Vuic, author of "Officer, Nurse, Woman:  the Army Nurse Corps in the
Vietnam War."  With the nation's great need for bodies to help, women
served for the first time as soldiers.  They didn't fight, but they
fixed trucks and tanks, ferried planes and performed all sorts of
other important jobs that had been previously closed off to them.
