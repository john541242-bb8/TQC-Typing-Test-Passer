     Levitated beings don't seem to suffer any ill effects.  In terms
of the test frog, Hill said, "It went back to its fellow frogs looking
perfectly happy."  Fruit flies are smaller, more common, and are easier
to deal with, however, so the success of this latest study suggests
they may be used again in future to find out how life forms respond to
an environment with a different gravitational field than Earth.
     Vacuums are supposed to make our lives cleaner, but some may do
just the opposite.  When Australian researchers looked at what was
stirred up by 21 vacuum cleaners, they found that all of the vacuums,
which were made by 11 manufacturers, spewed out at least some bacteria
and fine dust particles.  Overall, there was a huge range in how much
pollution came out of each vacuum, the researchers reported in the
journal Environmental Science & Technology.
     Pomrenke and his crewmates will be featured on the new Discovery

many days straight," he said.  Residents have enough heating oil to
keep their houses warm, but not having enough gas for vehicles would
