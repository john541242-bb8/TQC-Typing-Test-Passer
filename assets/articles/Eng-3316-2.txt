     Stand in front of your real or imagined threshold.  Close your
eyes and take a moment to really feel the seemingly endless stasis of
winter, the cold, the stillness, the lack of growth or movement.  Think
of any situation in your life that has felt this winter-like sense of
stuckness and deadness.  Take a moment to honor periods of rest, pause,
and stillness in your life.  Now imagine that your soul-season is
shifting; a warm breeze is beginning to move in your life, bringing
in seeds sprouting, twigs budding, leaves and flowers unfurling.
