     Neutron stars have been called the zombies of the cosmos, shining
on even though they're technically dead, and occasionally feeding on a
neighboring star if it gets too close.  They are born when a massive
star runs out of fuel and collapses under its own gravity, crushing the
matter in its core and blasting away its outer layers in a supernova
explosion that can outshine a billion suns.
