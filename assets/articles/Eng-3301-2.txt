     You can think of the OHI as the GDP of the ocean, Halpern explains.
The concept of gross domestic product was not invented until just
over 75 years ago.  Before then, there was no simple way to track
how well a nation's economy and its people were doing.  Now GDP is
cited all the time.  Perhaps the same will be true of OHI when the
60-scientist team publishes its final assessments early next year.
