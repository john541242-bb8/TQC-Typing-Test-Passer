     Surplus electricity from the grid produces the hydrogen for the
fuel cells, which is stored in tanks on board.  There are no diesel
engines and thus no emissions.  Further efficiencies come from the
shape of the hull and propellers.  Such vessels are designed for short
trips, where the energy requirements are not as large as for long-haul
shipping.  The total cost, FutureShip says, is only about 25 percent
more than a conventional ferry.

a time.  Previously, officials have said that the workers could be
rescued, if all goes well, in late October or early November.
