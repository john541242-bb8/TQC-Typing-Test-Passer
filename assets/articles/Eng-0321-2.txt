     Sea urchins greedily graze on kelp when otters are not around,
but in the presence of the predators, urchins hide in crevices and eat
the plant scraps.  More otters mean more kelp and since the plant
is particularly good at capturing carbon through photosynthesis, this
also could mean less CO2 in the atmosphere.  During photosynthesis,
plants like kelp absorb carbon dioxide, which along with water and
energy from the sun, they use to convert it to organic matter.
