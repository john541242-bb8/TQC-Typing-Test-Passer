     As a nor'easter bears down on the Sandy ravaged East Coast, a
System to provide accurate and timely disaster information is being
rolled out by Google in collaboration with the U.S. National Oceanic
and Atmospheric Administration National Weather Service and the U.S.
Geological Survey.  Google Public Alerts website features a map of
the U.S. with severe weather warnings labeled and notices about recent
earthquakes.  The site allows users to search for key terms to find
important information such as:  What's happening?  Where and when
will an event strike?  How severe will it be?
