     Molitor urged that people practice common sense in the heat:
stay hydrated, and listen to your body.  If you're healthy and want
to exercise, try to get in your workout in the morning or evening,
rather than the middle of the day.  If you take medications that are
diuretics, such as blood pressure medications, you will need to drink
even more that usual to stay hydrated, Molitor said.  In addition,
focus on aspects of your life you can control, Molitor said, and realize
that eventually, it will cool down.
