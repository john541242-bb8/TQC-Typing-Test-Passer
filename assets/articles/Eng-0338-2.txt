     New Ladybug is More Turtle than Bug:  A newly discovered insect
in the western United States is not just a new species, but an entirely
new genus, reports Reuters.  The classification is so unique as this
bug can tuck it's head into its throat similar to how a turtle can
hide its head to protect itself.
