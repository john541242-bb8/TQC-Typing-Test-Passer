     Early humans may have evolved as prey animals rather than as
predators, suggest the remains of our prehistoric primate ancestors that
were devoured by hungry birds and carnivorous mammals.  The discovery
of multiple de-fleshed, chomped and gnawed bones from the extinct
primates, which lived 16 to 20 million years ago on Rusinga Island,
Kenya, was announced today at the Society of Vertebrate Paleontology's
70th Anniversary Meeting in Pittsburgh.
